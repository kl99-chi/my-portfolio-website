Solo build · 2026 · FNB App of the Year Hackathon

ProofPath

A platform that gives young South Africans verified proof of their skills. 60.9% of people aged 15 to 24 were unemployed in Q1 2026 (Stats SA), and employers can't verify a CV claim. On ProofPath a real business reviews a real piece of work and verifies the skill.

  • Python
  • Flet 1.0
  • scrypt
  • JSON storage
  • Render
Live app Source code
  • Three trust levels: self-declared, evidence-backed and business-verified. Only a business can promote a skill to the top level.
  • Three independent state machines for skill trust, challenge status and submission status.
  • Security from the start: salted scrypt password hashing, constant-time comparison and role-based route guards.
  • 34 automated rule checks protect the trust and challenge rules.
